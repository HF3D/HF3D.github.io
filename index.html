About

My approach to animation centres on observing and understanding real-world movement. Whether refining motion capture data or animating from video reference, I focus on the weight, timing and subtle physical details that make a performance feel natural.

I am particularly interested in the challenge of translating real human movement into convincing digital performances. Through my studies in 3D Animation, I am continuing to develop my technical and creative skills, with a focus on character animation for games.

My goal is to contribute to collaborative productions where attention to detail, realism and strong animation fundamentals help create believable characters and immersive experiences.
